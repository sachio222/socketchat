# Acceptable use

socketchat is for private conversations between people who chose to talk to each other. Please use it that way.

Do not use socketchat to:

- share anything that is illegal where you or the server are, including any sexual content involving a minor;
- threaten, harass, stalk or expose anyone;
- plan or carry out violence, fraud, or attacks on other people's computers;
- spread malware.

If you run a server, you answer for what happens on it. You choose who gets a server invite, whether members can invite others, and when the server shuts down. Encryption keeps you from reading the messages your server stores, so tell the people you invite what you expect of them.

People who work on socketchat follow the code of conduct in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.
